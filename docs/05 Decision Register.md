## Evidence Blockers

- BuildSense: usage evidence, launch state, architecture story, and final media.
- Bookify: contributor evidence, live-site verification, tests, screenshots, and specific tradeoff.
- Blood Bank: teammate split, verified architecture, API/deployment status, and repository hygiene.
- How to Train Your AI: authored systems, team roles, asset provenance, and playable/video evidence.
- CinemaVerse: commit/PR evidence, seat and payment details, tests, and deployment state.
- Personal: current CV, verified contact links, education wording, portrait decision, and availability wording.

## Change Rule

When an open decision closes:

1. Move it into Confirmed Decisions with a date and reason.
2. Update the affected English documents.
3. Apply the equivalent change to the Arabic documents.
4. Update acceptance criteria or evidence blockers when required.
